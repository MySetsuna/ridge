# Eliminate residual monochrome glyph blur

The existing monochrome coverage transfer leaves roughly one quarter of
visible pixels in a grayscale transition band at DPR 1. System glyph geometry
is correct, but the broad fringe remains visibly softer than native terminal
text.

## Acceptance

- Narrow the centred monochrome coverage transfer without changing glyph
  geometry, cell widths, atlas sampling, or color emoji alpha.
- DPR 1 system-glyph screenshots retain CJK, symbols, emoji, and clusters with
  at least 80% solid monochrome coverage and at most 20% transition coverage.
- DPR 1.25 retains complete glyphs and stable geometry; its WebView2
  screencast is not used for pixel ratios because CDP downsamples the device
  frame to CSS dimensions.
- Screenshot analysis maps CSS coordinates to captured image pixels before
  extracting the fixture region.
- WebGPU and WebGL2 continue through the same GPU compositor.

Describe the approved semantic delta and its acceptance tests.
