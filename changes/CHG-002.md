# Remote public-network and physical-device evidence

## Remaining scope

- Record Remote latency, queue depth, teardown, reconnect, IME, and terminal
  rendering on a physical mobile device over a public network.
- Compare that evidence with the deterministic local mobile/WebGPU/WebGL2 gates.
- Keep this change draft until external evidence exists; local emulation does not
  satisfy `L4-REMOTE-PERFORMANCE-GATE-001`.

## Evidence required

- Public-network latency and reconnect trace.
- Physical-device WebGPU or WebGL2 backend, IME, resize, background/resume, and
  sustained terminal-output results.
- Bounded queue, timer, listener, transport, and heap baselines after teardown.

Agent activity, cards, desktop demand loading, and shared process discovery were
verified and closed by `CHG-001`.
