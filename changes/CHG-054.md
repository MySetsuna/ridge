## §4 Why CHG-054 is needed

Without this change:
- The 13 pre-existing diffs (all A-category, no functional risk) accumulate as
  `POLICY_E_UNAUTHORIZED_DIFF` errors in every `stc verify` run.
- CHG-052 cannot complete because verify fails on these paths.
- CHG-053 cannot lock because CHG-052 is stuck active.
- M2.1 cannot proceed because the governance debt blocks every subsequent change.

CHG-054 is the minimum, precise reconciliation change required to unblock the
project. It does not introduce product semantics; it only legitimizes already-
shipped work that lost its governance anchor.
